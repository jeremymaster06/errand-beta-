# Electronic Monitoring Notice: NY Civil Rights Law §52-c
> **Draft, pending attorney review. Not in effect. This is a demo; no service is offered.** [BRACKETS] = placeholders.

**Law:** Civ. Rights Law §52-c (eff. May 7, 2022), https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CVR/52-C. Requires: (1) written notice at hiring, (2) the employee's written or electronic acknowledgment, and (3) a notice posted in a conspicuous place that employees can easily see. Civil penalties: $500 first, $1,000 second, $3,000 each after that (enforced by the AG).
