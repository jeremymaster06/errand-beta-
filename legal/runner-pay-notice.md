## Legal requirements (Labor Law §195(1), https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/LAB/195)
- Give **at hire** (before work starts), and in writing **at least 7 days before** any rate change unless the change shows on the next wage statement (§195(2)).
- In **English and the employee's primary language**, if the NY DOL publishes a template in that language. DOL templates and translations: https://dol.ny.gov/pay-notice-and-record-keeping. If none exists, English alone is OK.
- Get a **signed, dated acknowledgment** stating the employee got it and their primary language. Keep it **6 years**.
- Penalty for not giving it: up to $50/day per employee, max $5,000 (Labor Law §198(1-b)).

## Which DOL form
**LS 54 and LS 56 don't appear to fit.** LS 54 is for hourly-rate employees and LS 56 for weekly-rate/salary for fixed hours. NY DOL's **LS 59** appears to cover "weekly rate or salary for varying hours, day rates, piece rates, flat rates or other non-hourly pay."
